Our Story

Who We Are

We're Sam and Nick Brusilow, founders of Westside Earthworks, an all-American grading, leveling, and site preparation business right here in Michigan. Proudly family owned and operated, we're dedicated to providing top-quality site solutions. Our business is licensed by the State of Michigan as a Residential Maintenance and Alterations Contractor (M&A) with classification D (Excavation).

Our Purpose

We believe in waking up early, working hard, staying organized, and responding quickly. Amid the current construction boom, we seek to stand out in professionalism, communcation, and value. We want you to be so satisfied with our work and service that you come back each and every time you need land preparation work done right.
